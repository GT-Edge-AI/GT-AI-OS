# Teams

Teams in GT AI OS enable collaborative work with shared agents, datasets, and resources. This guide explains how to work effectively with teams.

## Understanding Teams

### What is a Team?

A team is a group of users who:
- Share access to specific agents and datasets
- Collaborate on common projects or workflows
- Have defined roles and permissions within the team

### Team Benefits

Working in teams allows you to:
- Share custom agents with colleagues
- Collaborate on dataset curation
- Maintain consistent AI interactions across your group
- Control access to sensitive resources

## Joining Teams

### Team Invitations

When you're invited to a team:
1. A badge with a number appears on the **Teams** button in the sidebar
2. Click **Teams** to view your pending invitations
3. Review the invitation details
4. Click **Accept** or **Decline**

### Finding Your Teams

View your team memberships:
1. Go to **Teams** in the sidebar
2. See all teams you belong to
3. Click a team to view details

## Working with Team Resources

### Team Agents

Agents shared with your team appear on the Agents page. To use a team agent:
1. Go to the Agents page
2. Find the agent shared with your team
3. Click on the agent card to start a conversation
4. The agent has access to team datasets

### Team Datasets

Datasets shared with your team:
- Appear on the Datasets page
- Can be attached to your personal agents
- Are accessible in team agent configurations

### Sharing Your Resources

To share resources with your team:

**Sharing an Agent:**
1. Edit your agent
2. Set visibility to **Team**
3. Select the team(s) to share with
4. Save changes

**Sharing a Dataset:**
1. Edit your dataset
2. Set visibility to **Team**
3. Select the team(s) to share with
4. Save changes

## Team Roles

### Understanding Roles

| Role | Capabilities |
|------|-------------|
| Member (read) | Use shared resources, view team info |
| Contributor (share) | Use resources AND share own resources with the team |
| Manager | Manage members, invite users, view Observable activity |
| Owner | Full control, delete team, transfer ownership |

### Role-Based Access

Your role determines what you can do:

**Members can:**
- Use team agents and datasets
- View team membership
- Leave the team

**Contributors can additionally:**
- Share their own agents and datasets with the team

**Managers can additionally:**
- Invite new members
- Remove members
- Change member permissions
- View Observable activity for the team

**Owners can additionally:**
- Delete the team
- Transfer ownership
- Edit team settings

## Team Communication

### Best Practices for Team Collaboration

1. **Document your agents**: Add clear descriptions so team members understand when to use each agent

2. **Organize datasets**: Use consistent naming and tagging for team datasets

3. **Communicate changes**: Let team members know when you update shared resources

4. **Request feedback**: Ask teammates how agents and datasets are working for them

### Coordinating Agent Development

When creating agents for your team:
1. Discuss requirements with team members
2. Create a draft agent for testing
3. Gather feedback from the team
4. Iterate based on input
5. Set visibility to team when ready

## Managing Team Membership

### Inviting Members (Managers/Owners)

1. Go to your team's page
2. Click **Invite Members**
3. Enter email addresses
4. Select roles for new members
5. Send invitations

### Removing Members (Managers/Owners)

1. Go to your team's page
2. Find the member in the list
3. Click the remove option
4. Confirm removal

### Changing Permissions (Owners/Managers)

1. Go to your team's page
2. Find the member
3. Use the role dropdown to change their permission level
4. Changes are saved automatically

## Leaving a Team

To leave a team you belong to:
1. Go to **Teams** in the sidebar
2. Click on the team
3. Click **Leave Team**
4. Confirm your decision

**Note**: If you're the only owner, you must transfer ownership before leaving.

## Team Settings (Managers/Owners)

### Editing Team Information

1. Go to your team's page
2. Click **Settings**
3. Update team name or description
4. Save changes

### Configuring Team Preferences

You can configure:
- **Default resource permission**: Set whether new shared resources default to Read or Edit permissions

## Best Practices

### Organizing Team Resources

- Use clear naming conventions
- Add descriptive tags
- Document purpose and use cases
- Review and delete unused resources

### Onboarding New Members

When new members join:
1. Share documentation about team agents
2. Explain dataset organization
3. Point them to this help documentation
4. Offer to answer questions

### Resource Governance

- Regularly review who has access
- Delete resources no longer needed
- Update agents and datasets as requirements change
- Communicate significant changes to the team
