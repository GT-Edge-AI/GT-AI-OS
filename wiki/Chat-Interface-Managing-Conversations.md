# Managing Conversations

This guide covers how to manage your conversation history in GT AI OS.

## Conversation History

### Finding Past Conversations

Your conversation history appears in the sidebar. Use the filter dropdown to switch between time periods:

- **All**: View all conversations
- **Today**: Recent conversations from today
- **This Week**: Conversations from the past 7 days
- **This Month**: Conversations from the past 30 days

### Searching Conversations

Use the search bar in the conversation history to find past conversations by conversation **title**.

**Tip**: Give important conversations descriptive names to make them easier to find later.

### Conversation Details

Each conversation shows:
- The agent you were chatting with
- When the conversation was last active
- Message count

## Continuing Conversations

To resume a previous conversation:
1. Click on the conversation in the sidebar
2. The full history loads in the main area
3. Continue chatting where you left off

## Starting New Conversations

To start a fresh conversation:
1. Click **New Chat** in the top right of the chat header to start fresh with the same agent
2. Or click the **Bot icon** to go to the Agents page and select a different agent

**Note**: Starting a new conversation creates a separate thread. Your previous conversations remain accessible in the history.

## Conversation Organization

### Renaming Conversations

1. Hover over a conversation in the sidebar
2. Click the **More** (⋮) menu that appears
3. Select **Rename**
4. Enter a new name
5. Press Enter to save

### Deleting Conversations

1. Hover over a conversation in the sidebar
2. Click the **More** (⋮) menu that appears
3. Select **Delete**
4. Confirm the action

**Note**: Deleted conversations are removed from your view but retained for audit purposes.

## Best Practices

- Use descriptive names for important conversations
- Delete conversations you no longer need
- Start new conversations for new topics
- Use search to quickly find past discussions by title
