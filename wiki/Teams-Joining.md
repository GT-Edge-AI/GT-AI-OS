# Joining Teams

This guide explains how to join teams and accept team invitations in GT AI OS.

## Finding Team Invitations

### Sidebar Badge

When you're invited to a team:
1. A badge with a number appears on the **Teams** button in the sidebar
2. The number shows how many pending invitations you have
3. Click **Teams** to view and respond to invitations

### Teams Page

View all pending invitations:
1. Go to **Teams** in the sidebar
2. Pending invitations appear at the top of the page
3. Each invitation shows the team name and who invited you

## Accepting Invitations

### How to Accept

1. Find the invitation (via notification or Teams page)
2. Review the team details:
   - Team name and description
   - Who invited you
   - What role you're being offered
3. Click **Accept**
4. You're now a team member

### Declining Invitations

If you don't want to join:
1. Find the invitation
2. Click **Decline**
3. Optionally provide a reason

## Understanding Your Role

### Team Roles

When you join, you're assigned a role:

| Role | What You Can Do |
|------|-----------------|
| Member | Use shared resources, view team info |
| Contributor | Use resources AND share own resources with the team |
| Manager | Manage members, invite users, view Observable activity |
| Owner | Full control, delete team, transfer ownership |

### Role Assignments

- Your role is set by whoever invited you
- Typically new members start as Members
- Owners or Managers can change your role later

## Your First Steps as a Team Member

### 1. Explore Team Resources

After joining:
- Check what agents are shared
- Review available datasets
- Understand team workflows

### 2. Connect with Your Team

- Introduce yourself to team members
- Ask about team workflows and expectations
- Learn how the team uses shared resources

### 3. Get Oriented

- Read team documentation
- Ask team managers about best practices
- Understand team-specific guidelines

## Viewing Your Teams

### Finding Your Teams

1. Go to **Teams** in the sidebar
2. See all teams you belong to
3. Click a team to view details

### Team Details

Each team shows:
- Team name and description
- Member count
- Your role
- Shared resources

## Leaving a Team

### How to Leave

1. Go to **Teams** in the sidebar
2. Click on the team
3. Click **Leave Team**
4. Confirm your decision

### What Happens When You Leave

- You lose access to team resources
- Your personal resources remain yours
- Team resources you created stay with the team
- You can be re-invited later

### Ownership Consideration

If you're the only owner:
- You must transfer ownership before leaving
- Or promote another member to owner
- Teams need at least one owner

## Troubleshooting

### Invitation Not Appearing

- Look for the badge on the **Teams** button in the sidebar
- Verify the correct email was used for the invitation
- Ask the inviter to resend the invitation

### Can't Accept Invitation

- Ensure you're logged in
- Check if the invitation expired
- Contact the team admin

### Access Issues After Joining

- Give the system a moment to update
- Try logging out and back in
- Contact your team admin
